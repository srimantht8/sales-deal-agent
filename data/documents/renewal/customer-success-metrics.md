# Customer Success Metrics & Benchmarks

## Overview

This document provides benchmark metrics for measuring customer success with our platform. Use these during renewal discussions to demonstrate value and identify expansion opportunities.

## Platform Adoption Metrics

### Usage Benchmarks (Healthy Customer)

| Metric | Target | Top Quartile |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Monthly Active Users (% of licensed) | > 70% | > 90% |
| Workflows in production | > 5 | > 15 |
| Average executions/user/month | > 50 | > 200 |
| API integration count | > 3 | > 8 |
| Data sources connected | > 2 | > 5 |

### Engagement Health Signals

**Green (Healthy)**:
- Weekly active usage above 60%
- New workflows created monthly
- Support tickets trending down
- User count growing organically

**Yellow (Monitor)**:
- Usage between 40-60%
- No new workflows in 30+ days
- Increase in support tickets
- Key champion has changed roles

**Red (At Risk)**:
- Usage below 40%
- No new workflows in 60+ days
- Executive sponsor disengaged
- Competitor evaluation mentioned

## Business Impact Metrics

### Operational Efficiency
- **Process automation rate**: Percentage of manual steps eliminated
  - Benchmark: 65% automation rate within 6 months
- **Time savings**: Hours saved per user per month
  - Benchmark: 15 hours/user/month average
- **Error reduction**: Decrease in manual processing errors
  - Benchmark: 70% reduction in first year

### Financial Impact
- **Cost savings**: Direct labor and operational cost reduction
  - Benchmark: $500-$2,000/user/year in savings
- **Revenue impact**: Faster deal cycles, better customer targeting
  - Benchmark: 15-25% improvement in targeted metrics
- **Compliance savings**: Reduced audit prep and regulatory reporting costs
  - Benchmark: 60-80% reduction in compliance effort

## Renewal Health Indicators

### Strong Renewal Signals
- NPS score > 40
- Platform usage above 80% of licensed capacity
- Active expansion discussions
- Executive sponsor engaged
- Multiple departments using the platform

### Risk Indicators
- NPS score < 20
- Usage below 50% of licensed capacity
- Budget concerns raised
- Competitor mentions in conversations
- Key stakeholder departures

## QBR Framework

Quarterly Business Reviews should cover:

1. **Usage Dashboard**: MAU, workflow count, execution volume, trend charts
2. **Value Delivered**: Calculated savings, efficiency gains, error reduction
3. **Success Stories**: 2-3 specific examples of platform impact from the quarter
4. **Roadmap Alignment**: Upcoming features relevant to customer's use cases
5. **Expansion Opportunities**: Next use cases based on current usage patterns
6. **Action Items**: Mutual commitments for the next quarter

## Calculating Customer Health Score

```
Health Score = (Usage Score × 0.3) + (Engagement Score × 0.25) +
              (Value Score × 0.25) + (Relationship Score × 0.2)

Each component scored 0-100:
- Usage: MAU %, workflow count, execution volume
- Engagement: Support interactions, QBR attendance, feature requests
- Value: Realized ROI vs projected, user satisfaction
- Relationship: Executive access, champion stability, NPS
```
