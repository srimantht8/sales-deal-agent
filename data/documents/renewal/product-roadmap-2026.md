# Product Roadmap 2026

## Vision

Our 2026 roadmap focuses on three pillars: **Autonomous AI Workflows**, **Enterprise Collaboration**, and **Industry-Specific Solutions**. These investments build on our core platform strengths and directly respond to customer feedback.

## Q2 2026 (April - June)

### Autonomous Workflow Agents
- **Self-healing workflows**: Agents automatically detect and recover from failures without human intervention
- **Multi-agent orchestration**: Support for complex workflows with multiple AI agents collaborating on tasks
- **Adaptive learning**: Workflows that improve performance based on historical execution data

### Enhanced RAG Capabilities
- **Multi-modal RAG**: Support for images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and video content in retrieval workflows
- **Hybrid search**: Combine vector similarity with keyword and metadata filtering
- **Dynamic re-indexing**: Automatic document re-embedding when source content changes

## Q3 2026 (July - September)

### Enterprise Collaboration Hub
- **Shared workflow marketplace**: Publish and discover workflows across teams
- **Collaborative editing**: Real-time multi-user workflow design
- **Version control**: Git-like workflow versioning with diff, merge, and rollback

### Code Quality Module (NEW)
- **Code intelligence**: AI-powered code review, bug detection, and refactoring suggestions
- **DevOps integration**: Automated CI/CD pipeline optimization
- **Technical debt scoring**: Quantify and prioritize technical debt across repositories
- *Available as add-on module*

## Q4 2026 (October - December)

### Industry Solutions
- **Industry-specific solution packages**: Pre-built workflows tailored to healthcare, financial services, supply chain, and other verticals
- **Domain-specific models**: Fine-tuned models optimized for regulated industry terminology and compliance requirements
- **Vertical templates**: Ready-to-deploy workflow templates for common industry use cases

### Advanced Analytics
- **AI performance dashboard**: Track model accuracy, latency, and cost across all workflows
- **Business impact metrics**: Connect workflow execution to business KPIs
- **Custom reporting**: Build and schedule reports with natural language queries

## Long-Term Direction (2027+)

- Edge deployment support for latency-sensitive applications
- Federated learning for privacy-preserving model training
- Natural language workflow creation ("Build me a workflow that...")
- Expanded marketplace with third-party workflow templates

## For Existing Customers

Renewal customers receive:
- **Early access** to all roadmap features (beta program)
- **Migration support** for new capabilities at no additional cost
- **Dedicated roadmap review** sessions with product team
- **Influence on priorities** through Customer Advisory Board
