# ROI Calculator Methodology

## Framework Overview

This document outlines our methodology for calculating return on investment for enterprise data platform deployments. Use these frameworks during pricing discussions to help customers quantify the value proposition.

## ROI Formula

```
ROI = (Total Benefits - Total Costs) / Total Costs × 100%

Total Benefits = Labor Savings + Error Reduction + Revenue Acceleration + Compliance Savings
Total Costs = License Fees + Implementation + Training + Ongoing Operations
```

## Benefit Categories

### 1. Labor Savings (typically 40-60% of total benefits)
- **Metric**: Hours saved per user per month × fully loaded hourly rate × number of users
- **Benchmark**: Average 15 hours/user/month for automated workflows
- **Example**: 200 users × 15 hours × $75/hour × 12 months = **$2.7M/year**

### 2. Error Reduction (typically 15-25% of total benefits)
- **Metric**: Current error rate × cost per error × volume of transactions
- **Benchmark**: AI-driven processes reduce error rates by 60-80%
- **Example**: 10,000 monthly transactions × 5% error rate × $200/error × 70% reduction = **$840K/year**

### 3. Revenue Acceleration (typically 10-20% of total benefits)
- **Metric**: Faster deal cycles, improved lead scoring, better customer targeting
- **Benchmark**: 20-30% reduction in sales cycle length
- **Example**: $50M pipeline × 25% faster close × 10% win rate improvement = **$1.25M/year**

### 4. Compliance Savings (typically 5-15% of total benefits)
- **Metric**: Audit preparation time, regulatory reporting, fine avoidance
- **Benchmark**: 80% reduction in compliance reporting effort
- **Example**: 2 FTEs dedicated to compliance × $150K fully loaded × 80% automation = **$240K/year**

## Cost Components

| Component | Typical Range | Notes |
|-----------|---------------|-------|
| License Fees | $200K-$1.2M/year | Depends on tier and seats |
| Implementation | $50K-$200K one-time | Professional services |
| Training | $10K-$50K one-time | Included in Enterprise tier |
| Ongoing Ops | 10-15% of license/year | Customer-side admin, not our cost |

## How to Use in Proposals

1. Gather customer-specific data during discovery
2. Apply industry benchmarks where customer data is unavailable
3. Present conservative, moderate, and optimistic scenarios
4. Include sensitivity analysis on key assumptions
5. Tie ROI to customer's stated business objectives
