# Enterprise Pricing Guide

## Pricing Tiers

Our pricing is designed to scale with your usage while providing predictable costs for budget planning.

### Tier 1: Starter
- **$500/month** base + $50/user/month
- Up to 50 users
- 100,000 workflow executions/month
- Standard support (business hours)
- Best for: Proof of concept and small teams

### Tier 2: Professional
- **$2,000/month** base + $35/user/month
- Up to 500 users
- 1,000,000 workflow executions/month
- Priority support (24/5)
- SSO and audit logging included
- Best for: Department-level deployments

### Tier 3: Enterprise
- **Custom pricing** based on deployment model
- Unlimited users
- Unlimited executions
- 24/7 premium support with dedicated CSM
- VPC deployment option
- Custom SLA (up to 99.99%)
- Best for: Organization-wide rollouts, regulated industries
