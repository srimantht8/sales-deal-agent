## Key Differentiators

### vs. Vendor A (Data Engineering-Focused)
- We focus on AI workflow orchestration; they focus on data engineering pipelines
- Our platform includes native human-in-the-loop capabilities
- Faster time-to-value: pre-built workflow templates vs. custom notebook development
- Complementary, not competitive — we integrate with data engineering platforms as a data source

### vs. Vendor B (Government/Defense-Focused)
- Significantly lower total cost of ownership
- More flexible model choices (not locked into a proprietary ecosystem)
- Faster deployment (weeks vs. months)
- More developer-friendly with code-first approach alongside visual tools

### vs. Vendor C (Industry AI Platform)
- Broader AI model support beyond proprietary models
- Better real-time processing capabilities
- More mature enterprise RAG and document intelligence
- Stronger cloud partner ecosystem

### vs. Building In-House
- 10x faster time to market
- Built-in security, compliance, and monitoring
- Ongoing updates and model support without maintenance burden
- Typical 60-70% lower 3-year TCO compared to in-house development

## Common Evaluation Criteria

When evaluating platforms, we recommend customers assess:

1. **Integration Speed**: How quickly can you connect to existing data sources?
2. **Model Flexibility**: Can you switch between AI models without re-architecting?
3. **Security Posture**: Does the platform meet your compliance requirements out of the box?
4. **Total Cost of Ownership**: Include ongoing maintenance, not just license fees
5. **Time to First Value**: How quickly can you deliver a working proof of concept?
