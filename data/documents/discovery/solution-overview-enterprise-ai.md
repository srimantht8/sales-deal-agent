# Solution Overview: Enterprise Data Platform

## What We Do

Our platform enables organizations to build, deploy, and scale AI-powered workflows across their business operations. Unlike point solutions, our platform provides a unified foundation for multiple use cases — from customer intelligence to operational optimization.

## Core Capabilities

### Intelligent Workflow Automation
- Build complex multi-step AI workflows with visual designer and code-first options
- Orchestrate across data sources, models, and business systems
- Human-in-the-loop approval for high-stakes decisions

### Enterprise Data Integration
- Pre-built connectors for 50+ enterprise systems including major CRM, ERP, data warehouse, and cloud platforms
- Real-time and batch data ingestion
- Built-in data quality and governance

### AI Model Management
- Support for leading foundation models with flexible model selection
- Fine-tuning and RAG capabilities for domain-specific accuracy
- Model performance monitoring and automated retraining

### Security & Compliance
- SOC 2 Type II certified
- HIPAA, GDPR, and CCPA compliant deployment options
- VPC deployment with customer-managed encryption keys
- Complete audit trail and explainability

## Deployment Options

| Option | Description | Best For |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Cloud SaaS | Fully managed, multi-tenant | Fast deployment, smaller teams |
| Dedicated Cloud | Single-tenant in our infrastructure | Mid-market, moderate compliance |
| VPC / On-Premise | Deploy in customer's infrastructure | Regulated industries, strict data residency |
