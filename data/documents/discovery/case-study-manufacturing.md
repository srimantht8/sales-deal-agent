# Case Study: TerraForm Manufacturing — AI-Driven Production Optimization

## Overview

TerraForm Manufacturing, a mid-market industrial manufacturer with $2B in annual revenue, deployed our platform to optimize their production line scheduling and predictive maintenance workflows.

## Challenge

TerraForm faced mounting pressure from supply chain disruptions and rising operational costs. Their legacy scheduling system relied on manual spreadsheets, leading to 15% production downtime and $12M in annual waste. They evaluated build-vs-buy approaches and multiple vendors including incumbent analytics tools and custom-built solutions before selecting our platform.

## Solution

We deployed our platform with custom integrations into their existing ERP system and IoT sensor network. Key components included:

- **Predictive Maintenance Models**: ML models trained on 3 years of sensor data to predict equipment failures 72 hours in advance
- **Dynamic Scheduling Engine**: AI-optimized production scheduling that adapts to real-time demand signals
- **Quality Control Automation**: Computer vision models for defect detection on the production line

## Results

Within 6 months of deployment:

- **40% reduction** in unplanned downtime
- **$8.2M annual savings** from optimized scheduling
- **22% improvement** in overall equipment effectiveness (OEE)
- **3.2x ROI** achieved in first year

> "The platform paid for itself in the first quarter. What used to take our team days of manual planning now happens automatically with better results." — VP of Operations, TerraForm Manufacturing
