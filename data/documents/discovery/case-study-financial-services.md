# Case Study: Pinnacle Capital — Enterprise Analytics for Risk Management

## Overview

Pinnacle Capital, a Fortune 500 financial services firm managing $180B in assets, adopted our platform to modernize their risk analytics and compliance workflows.

## Challenge

Pinnacle's risk team was processing 10,000+ daily transactions using legacy rule-based systems that produced high false-positive rates (68%) and required extensive manual review. Regulatory pressure from SEC and FINRA demanded faster, more accurate risk assessments.

## Solution

Our platform was deployed in a phased approach:

- **Phase 1 (Month 1-2)**: Data integration with existing cloud data warehouse and trading systems
- **Phase 2 (Month 3-4)**: Custom risk scoring models trained on 5 years of transaction history
- **Phase 3 (Month 5-6)**: Real-time alerting and automated compliance reporting

The solution runs entirely within Pinnacle's private cloud environment, meeting strict data residency and regulatory requirements.

## Results

After 8 months in production:

- **False positive rate reduced from 68% to 12%** — saving 200+ analyst hours per week
- **Real-time risk scoring**: Transaction risk assessments now complete in <100ms (down from 4 hours)
- **Automated compliance reports**: SEC/FINRA reporting that previously took 2 weeks now generated in hours
- **$15M annual cost savings** from reduced manual review and faster processing

## Key Takeaway

For financial services companies in the discovery phase, this case study demonstrates our platform's ability to handle sensitive financial data at scale while meeting regulatory requirements. The phased deployment approach minimizes risk and delivers value incrementally.
